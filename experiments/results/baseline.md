## 2. Key Findings & Biological Observations

1. **Lifespan Under Random Walk:**
   - An agent taking uniform random actions survives on average **2333.33 ticks per life** before succumbing to starvation and dehydration.
   - Over 5,000 ticks, a random agent dies on average **1.3 times** (roughly once every 2333.33 ticks).
2. **Accidental Food Consumption:**
   - Across 20 seeds, random agents ate an average of **7.05 food items** over 5,000 ticks. The probability of randomly standing next to an edible fruit, facing it, and emitting the `EAT` action ID without dying of thirst first is near zero.
3. **State-Action Coverage:**
   - A random agent explores on average **4180 distinct state-action-outcome transitions** per 5,000 ticks. However, these transitions remain concentrated in basic movement and wall collisions, rarely discovering multi-step causal chains.
4. **Causal Chain Failure:**
   - The complex sequence: `PICK_UP(seed) -> MOVE(to soil) -> USE_ON_FACING(plant) -> WAIT/WATER -> PICK_UP(fruit) -> EAT` was never observed end-to-end under random action selection.
